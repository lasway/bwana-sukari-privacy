Privacy Policy — Bwana Sukari apps

Covers Bwana Sukari REWARDZ (com.rewardzret) and Bwana Sukari Field (com.rewardzfield).

Last updated: 1 October 2026

BRAMEX SOLUTIONS LIMITED (“we”, “us”) operates the Bwana Sukari REWARDZ and Bwana Sukari Field mobile applications. This policy explains what personal data the apps collect, why we collect it, how it is protected, and how you can ask us to delete it.

What we collect and why

DataWhy we collect itApps
Phone numberTo create and secure your account and sign you in with a one-time code (OTP).Both

We do not collect your name list / contacts, financial or payment information, or government identifiers.

How your data is used

We use the data only to operate the Bwana Sukari loyalty and field programmes — verifying shops and visits, awarding points and rewards, sending relevant notifications, and providing support.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it with third parties for advertising. Your data is stored on our own servers and transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and associated personal data at any time by emailing krishon@bramex.co.tz. We respond to deletion requests within 30 days.

Children

These apps are business tools intended for shopkeepers and field staff aged 18 and over, and are not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date above reflects the latest version.
